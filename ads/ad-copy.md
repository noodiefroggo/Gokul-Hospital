## Notes for the media buyer

- Keep text on the image light. The creative carries only a headline, one line of support and the buttons, which matches Meta's guidance.
- The in-image "WhatsApp us / Call now" buttons echo Meta's native CTA. Meta still renders its own CTA bar below the image.
- Healthcare ads: Meta's personal-attributes policy flags copy that asserts or implies the viewer is pregnant ("Are you pregnant?", "Expecting?"). The lines above describe the service instead of the viewer. Keep any edits that way. Note that "Book **your** pregnancy check-up" (the requested headline, also on the creative) could be read that way too. If review rejects it, swap to "Book a pregnancy check-up" in both the headline field and the image.
- Replace the labelled photo placeholder with real, consented hospital photography before running.
