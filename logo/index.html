Gokul Hospital · Identity
Logo proposal · Concept presentation

A feather, held gently.

Gokul is where Krishna was raised by Yashoda, so the name already means a mother's care. The mark borrows only his peacock feather: a calm, watchful eye of navy, teal and gold, set in a vane soft enough to feel like an embrace. माँ जैसी देखभाल, care like a mother's.

01 · Concepts

Three routes, one symbol

Each route keeps the reference to Krishna symbolic and quiet, so the logo reads as a hospital first. All three share the same wordmark: Fraunces for GOKUL, with HOSPITAL set wide in Manrope beneath a temple-gold hairline.

CONCEPT 01

Mor Pankh Final

A single peacock feather, leaning gently toward the name. A calm eye of navy, teal and gold, with hairline barbs falling from the quill.

Why it wins: the most calm and clinical of the three, and the only one that still reads clearly at 16px.

CONCEPT 02

Pankh & Bansuri

The feather crossing Krishna's flute. It is the most explicit nod to Gokul and carries a sense of music and celebration.

Not chosen: the flute pulls it toward devotional iconography, and the holes disappear at small sizes.

CONCEPT 03

Soft Cross

The same feather, but the eye's navy core becomes a soft, round-ended cross, so care sits at the very centre of the symbol.

Not chosen: it says hospital fastest, but the cross reads like a UI "add" button and loses the eye's softness.

02 · The mark

Anatomy of the feather

The feather is built symmetrically on one axis, then leaned 16 degrees toward the name. The eye sits slightly low, as it does in a real feather. Every part is a true vector shape, so the mark stays crisp from a favicon to a building sign.

  1. The eye, navy core

    A watchful, steady centre. Navy is the colour of trust and the primary text colour.

  2. Teal ring

    The feather's own colour, used across the brand as its primary accent.

  3. Temple-gold ring

    A thin band of warmth and heritage that frames the eye. It returns as the hairline in the wordmark.

  4. Vane and barbs

    Peacock blue deepening to teal. Nine pairs of hairline barbs taper down the quill, so the lower edge dissolves into feathering.

03 · Final system

05 · Clear space

Room to breathe

Give the logo the same calm the hospital promises. Keep the area around it free of text, edges and other graphics.

X = width of the eye

The clear space on every side equals the width of the feather's gold eye ring at the size in use.

Minimum sizes

Horizontal lockup: 120px or 30mm wide. Stacked: 72px or 18mm. Below 48px, use the favicon mark alone.

Don't

Don't recolour, rotate, stretch, outline or add effects to the mark, and don't place the colour version on teal or busy photos.
