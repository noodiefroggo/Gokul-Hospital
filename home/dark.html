Maternity

Your pregnancy journey, held at every step.

The same familiar faces, from planning a family to bringing your newborn home.

  1. Before

    Planning

    Pre-pregnancy and fertility consultations, including IVF when you need it.

  2. Weeks 1–12

    First trimester

    Your first check-ups, confirming the pregnancy and setting up your care plan.

  3. Weeks 13–27

    Second trimester

    Regular antenatal check-ups that follow your health and your baby's growth.

  4. Weeks 28–40

    Third trimester

    Preparing for birth together: normal delivery or C-section, whichever is safest.

  5. The big day

    Delivery

    A 24×7 team and operation theatres on hand, day or night.

  6. After

    Newborn & you

    NICU support if your baby needs it, and care for you as you recover.
