01 · The idea

The name already tells the story.

Gokul is where Krishna grew up in Yashoda's care. For families in Delhi, the name already means a mother's love and protection, and nurturing a child into the world.

That fits a hospital known for maternity, gynaecology and fertility care. So the brand doesn't invent a new promise. It names the one patients already describe in their reviews.

Krishna appears only as a symbol: his peacock feather, the mor pankh. It's warm and recognisable, and it stays calm enough for a clinical setting.

Warm

Cream, soft arches and a serif voice. It should feel like being looked after, not processed.

Trusted

4.8★ from 500+ ratings, open 24×7 since 2018. Proof sits up front on every page.

Close by

Opposite Metro Pillar 781, one tap to call or WhatsApp. Getting help should feel easy.

02 · Identity

One feather, held gently.

A single peacock feather, leaning toward the name. Its eye of navy, teal and gold still reads as a 16px browser icon. GOKUL is set in a soft serif, with HOSPITAL spaced out beneath a gold rule.
